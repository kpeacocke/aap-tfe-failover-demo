# Code of conduct

Help keep this project useful and welcoming:

- Be respectful, constructive and specific when discussing technical work.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, or share another person's private
  information or customer material.
- Assume good intent, accept feedback, and focus disagreement on behavior and
  evidence rather than individuals.
- Keep credentials, customer environments and security reports private.

Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate content, close discussions or
restrict participation where needed to protect contributors. Raise conduct
concerns privately with the repository owner through an established contact,
or use GitHub's platform reporting tools when no private contact is available.
Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.
